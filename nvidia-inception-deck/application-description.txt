Orkinosai Limited is building AI products for language-specific and regulated environments. Our near-term product is Ebru, a Turkish-first assistant designed for private deployment, governed workflows, and institutional use cases such as knowledge access, document work, and customer interaction. Our longer-term platform, Supreme ModelTX, is an early-stage sovereign model stack with repo-backed training, evaluation, checkpointed inference, and audit/governance components already in place. We plan to use NVIDIA GPUs and software to validate canonical training runs, expand Turkish-language model experiments, and harden secure inference for deployable AI systems.
