  Step 1: Dana's age = Emery's age + 6 = 12 + 6.
  Step 2: 12 + 6 = 18.
Answer: Dana is 18 years old.

Problem: Frankie scored 25% of 230 points on a quiz. How many points did Frankie score?
Solution:

  Step 2: Multiply: 230 × 0.25 = 57.
Answer: Frankie scored 57 points.
