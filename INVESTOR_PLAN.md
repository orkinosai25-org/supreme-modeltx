# Supreme Model TX — Investor & Resource Plan

**Company:** Orkinos AI Ltd (UK)

**Programme:** Supreme Model TX (SMTX)

**Document status:** Investor discussion draft — September 2026

## Important scope and assumptions

This document describes an early-stage UK sovereign-AI programme. It is intended to support an initial investor discussion and resource-sizing exercise; it is not a financial forecast, valuation, offer of securities, guarantee of returns, or claim that any potential commercial or infrastructure partner is currently contracted.

The programme is not yet a completed large-scale foundation model. The initial objective is to validate the architecture, build a capable UK-based team, secure appropriate compute, complete independent evaluation and security work, and progress toward credible institutional or enterprise pilots.

## 1. Executive summary

Orkinos AI Ltd is a UK-founded company developing Supreme Model TX, a British sovereign-AI programme focused on secure, controllable and strategically independent artificial-intelligence capability.

The programme is intended to support UK institutions, regulated sectors and strategic enterprises that require stronger control over deployment, data governance, security, evaluation and operational infrastructure. The long-term programme may include model and platform capabilities, but development will be staged around measurable technical and commercial milestones.

The immediate funding objective is to establish a focused technical team, provision secure compute, validate the architecture, build evaluation and governance capability, and deliver demonstrable pilot use cases.

## 2. What we are building

The programme has two connected workstreams:

### A. Sovereign AI platform and deployment capability

- Secure, institution-ready AI application and service architecture
- Controlled deployment in appropriate UK or customer-managed environments
- OpenAI-compatible service interfaces where useful for integration
- Retrieval-augmented generation and document-grounded workflows where appropriate
- Source citation, auditability, administration and access control
- Monitoring, evaluation, cost controls and operational governance
- Infrastructure-as-code and repeatable deployment processes

### B. Supreme Model TX research and model capability

- Model and system architecture research
- Data preparation and governance
- Fine-tuning, adaptation and controlled experimentation
- Benchmarking, safety testing and independent evaluation
- Inference optimisation and cost/performance analysis
- Security, robustness and misuse-resistance work
- Progressive assessment of whether larger-scale training is justified by evidence

The programme will not assume that full foundation-model pretraining is the first step. Compute-intensive activities will be staged according to validated requirements, technical results and commercial evidence.

## 3. Current stage

Supreme Model TX is at an early development and validation stage. The direction and strategic rationale are defined, while the next phase requires formal team formation, detailed technical planning, compute provisioning, evaluation design and pilot development.

The first capital tranche should create evidence for the next decision rather than commit immediately to the most expensive possible training path.

## 5. Compute and infrastructure requirements

Compute requirements depend heavily on whether the programme is conducting evaluation, retrieval and fine-tuning, or attempting large-scale pretraining. These are different cost profiles.

### Lean validation and pilot path

Indicative planning range:

- GPU and ML compute: **£30,000–£60,000 per month** during active experimentation
- General cloud infrastructure, storage, networking and monitoring: **£5,000–£15,000 per month**
- Security, logging, backup and data-governance services: included in the infrastructure plan and refined during technical design

This path is appropriate for architecture validation, fine-tuning, evaluation, pilot development and cost/performance analysis.

### Larger training or sustained research path

A larger training programme could require **£80,000–£200,000 or more per month**, depending on model size, hardware availability, utilisation, training duration, data pipeline requirements, storage and redundancy. The upper range should not be treated as a committed budget until an experienced training team has produced a compute plan.

Potential infrastructure approaches include:

- UK cloud or managed GPU providers
- Reserved or committed capacity where economically justified
- Spot or interruptible capacity for suitable experiments
- Dedicated clusters or colocation if utilisation supports the investment
- Partnerships with infrastructure and managed-service providers

Any proposed partnership with a named supplier would remain subject to commercial due diligence and written agreement.

## 7. Proposed use of funds

The initial funding would be allocated across five areas:

1. **People:** AI/ML, platform, data, evaluation, infrastructure, security and product capability.
2. **Compute:** GPU access, experimentation, fine-tuning, evaluation and inference capacity.
3. **Security and governance:** threat modelling, secure development, data governance, auditability and independent testing.
4. **Pilot delivery:** customer discovery, integration, deployment, support and measurement of real-world use cases.
5. **Company and IP infrastructure:** legal advice, IP protection, contracts, finance, insurance and operating controls.

A detailed budget and hiring plan should be approved against milestones rather than spending the full amount automatically.

## 8. Initial milestones

### Months 0–3: Foundation

- Confirm company, IP and governance structure
- Finalise technical architecture and threat model
- Recruit the initial engineering and research team
- Provision secure development and compute environments
- Define evaluation benchmarks and success criteria
- Identify qualified pilot prospects

### Months 3–6: Demonstration and validation

- Deliver the first integrated technical demonstration
- Establish data, evaluation and monitoring pipelines
- Complete initial security and performance assessment
- Produce cost/performance results for alternative model approaches
- Agree the scope of the first institutional or enterprise pilot

### Months 6–9: Pilot readiness

- Harden deployment and operational controls
- Run controlled pilot or proof-of-value activity
- Measure quality, latency, cost, security and user outcomes
- Decide whether additional fine-tuning or larger training is justified
- Prepare evidence for follow-on funding or strategic partnerships

### Months 9–12: Commercial and investment evidence

- Report pilot outcomes and lessons learned
- Secure initial commercial, institutional or strategic commitments where possible
- Refine the product and research roadmap
- Produce the next-stage technical and financial plan
- Prepare for follow-on capital if the evidence supports it

## 10. Investment discussion points

Before agreeing any structure, the following should be documented in writing:

- Whether the investment is into Orkinos AI Ltd or a new company
- Capital amount, currency, payment schedule and funding conditions
- Valuation, equity percentage and dilution mechanics
- Ownership and licensing of Supreme Model TX intellectual property
- Board composition, voting rights and reserved matters
- Founder role, compensation and vesting terms
- Treatment of future financing and investor participation rights
- Milestones, reporting obligations and use-of-funds controls
- What happens if funding stops or the relationship ends
- Confidentiality, exclusivity, non-compete and termination provisions

No return, valuation or exit outcome is guaranteed. The potential investor upside depends on successful technical delivery, customer adoption, revenue, strategic value and future financing or acquisition opportunities.

The investment case is to convert that experience, network and strategic opportunity into measurable evidence: a capable team, secure infrastructure, validated technical performance, credible pilots and a defensible UK AI company.
